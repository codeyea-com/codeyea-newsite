Frequently asked questions.

What services does CODEYEA provide?

CODEYEA provides AI and business automation, SEO and Generative Engine Optimization, website and application development, e-commerce solutions, digital marketing, brand design and graphic design.

How do you determine which services a business needs?

We begin with a discovery and assessment stage to understand the business, audience, current digital presence, internal processes and growth goals before recommending a suitable combination of services.

Can multiple services be combined into one project?

Yes. Services can be delivered individually or combined into one connected project, such as a website with SEO and GEO foundations, AI automation, brand design and digital marketing support.

What can AI and business automation do for my company?

AI and automation can help organize business information, support customers, automate repetitive tasks, connect workflows and create faster access to the information teams need. The solution depends on the company’s actual processes and requirements.

What is the difference between SEO and GEO?

SEO improves visibility in traditional search engines. Generative Engine Optimization helps structure and strengthen content so it can be understood and referenced by AI-powered search and answer platforms. Both should work together as part of a modern visibility strategy.

Can CODEYEA improve an existing website, application or digital system?

Yes. We can review an existing platform and recommend whether targeted improvements, integrations, automation or a broader rebuild would be the more practical approach.

How long does a project take?

The timeline depends on the project scope, technical requirements, content readiness, integrations and review process. A clear delivery schedule is prepared after discovery and scope confirmation.

What happens after launch or delivery?

We complete the agreed handoff, confirm that the delivered solution has been tested and provide any included documentation or support. Maintenance, optimization, marketing and future development can be arranged separately.
