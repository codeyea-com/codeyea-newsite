# Proposed support packages

Research date: 2026-09-27. Private proposal, not approved commercial terms.

References:
- https://cohosta.com/wordpress-technical-support-services/ lists $50/month with setup fee and $250/month higher tier; includes maintenance and separately scoped assistance.
- https://wpbuffs.com/plans/ lists Maintain $89, Protect $179, Perform $239 per month. Maintenance, security and performance are differentiated; each plan covers one site.
- https://help.codeable.io/en/articles/368550-can-codeable-help-with-my-project lists $80–$120/hour as a basis for scoped work, varying with complexity and urgency.

CODEYEA proposal: Care Essential $49/month with 30 minutes minor fixes; Care Plus $129/month with 2 hours; both require CODEYEA hosting. Technical Rescue $75/hour for any host, one-hour minimum then half-hour increments. These prices and time allowances are proposed business decisions, not facts supplied by competitors. No guaranteed response time or recovery outcome.

Buttons open the existing support request portal; no checkout product or subscription backend was created. Tab CTAs navigate to matching plan cards.
