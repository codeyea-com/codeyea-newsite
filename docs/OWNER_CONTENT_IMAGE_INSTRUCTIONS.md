# CODEYEA content and imagery instructions

Owner instructions consolidated on 2026-10-12. These apply to every page and both Arabic and English. Latest explicit owner requests supersede earlier pauses or SEO-only trial scope.

## Meaning first
- Study each page and section's actual content, audience, service and business benefit before choosing imagery.
- Across the site, communicate what CODEYEA implements, the problem solved and the client's benefit: discoverability, reach, usability, efficient operations, enquiries, sales opportunities and technical improvement.
- Each image communicates its own section. Do not force the entire problem/implementation/outcome sequence into one image.
- Use meaningful real-life visual metaphors, such as a winning runner for improved ranking or a leading racing car for speed and performance, combined with relevant interfaces and data.
- Avoid unrelated product photos, generic office people, random packing scenes, toy technical sculptures and screenshots used merely to fill space.

## Visual references and variety
- Use the owner's full collection of references, not one repeated composition. Review Hostinger and SiteGround equivalent specialist pages and credible specialist competitors where topics differ.
- Target approximately 50% technical compositions and 50% real-life scenes/people across the page imagery collection. Technical designs can show a website with realistic Google results or diagnostic interfaces emerging as popups.
- Real-life compositions also fit industry pages when they convey the business and section message.
- Floating elements must genuinely protrude beyond the photo/frame. Space around the protruding portion must have real alpha transparency, so the section background shows through. Do not bake the whole composition into an opaque rectangular photographic canvas.
- Use broad diffuse shadows for depth and slightly translucent white interface panels (only slight transparency). Apply this selectively as content and section composition require.
- Image colors have creative freedom, not only CODEYEA colors. Preserve sharp UI/frame corners (border-radius:0).

## Dimensions before generation
- Inspect the real page before generation. Record each slot's width, height, aspect ratio, orientation, composition and relevant responsive behavior.
- Generate directly for the required measured size and orientation. Portrait slots remain portrait; landscape slots remain landscape. Preserve the existing section composition and dimensions.
- Plan necessary responsive variants before generation. Do not generate the wrong orientation and then repeatedly regenerate to correct avoidable mistakes.
- Account for protruding UI and shadows in the complete composition footprint. Do not cut important objects or text, shrink a meaningful subject inside embedded margins, stretch a photo, add blank padding or change the section to suit an image.
- Verify each image in its full section beside its title and copy; compare reference and implementation in equivalent frames. Automated dimension/load checks are not evidence of visual quality or approval.

## Photographic quality and originality
- Require credible skin, hair, leather, wood, fabric, glass and metal texture with natural detail and lighting.
- Whites must read neutral white. Avoid beige/off-white color washes, painted faces/hair, plastic skin or synthetic global color grading. Natural material shading and shadows are allowed.
- Every distinct placement needs a distinct image. Never repeat the hero in the CTA. Translations and responsive variants of the same placement may share the same concept.
- Do not claim generated demonstration graphics are actual verified client results. Do not invent testimonials, real client identities or performance statistics.
