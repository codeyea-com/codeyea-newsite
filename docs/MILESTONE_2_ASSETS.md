All three service-flow panels are fully readable. Portfolio and industry regions use native horizontal scrolling, not a carousel. No GSAP, Embla, autoplay, word rotation, counter animation, pointer enhancement, quote integration or deployment is included. Unavailable destinations are plain explanatory text rather than dead links.
