### sections.0.body

Before: Patients often begin with practical questions: what care is available, who provides it and how to arrange an appointment. A healthcare website should help people find those answers without making them work through unfamiliar language or confusing navigation.

Clear service information, approved professional profiles and accessible contact options support an informed first conversation. Educational content needs a review process, while any form collecting personal information needs an appropriate purpose and careful handling.

CODEYEA connects website design, content organization and search visibility around that patient journey. We help practices explain their services and assess suitable scheduling or communication integrations against their existing systems and responsibilities.

### sections.6.items.0.body

Before: We first review the existing provider, available integration methods and the practice’s requirements. Suitable connections can then be scoped without replacing systems that already serve the team well.

After: We evaluate the selected third-party system, its supported integration methods and the agreed project scope first. Appointment-system connections are implemented only when those requirements support them; they are not a default feature.

### sections.6.items.1.body

Before: We plan data collection, access and retention with the practice and its responsible advisers. Sensitive information should use an appropriate approved system; a general website form is not automatically suitable for clinical records.

After: We use privacy-conscious design and appropriate security safeguards based on the client’s selected systems, jurisdiction and project requirements. Sensitive information should use an approved system; a general inquiry form is not a medical-record system.
