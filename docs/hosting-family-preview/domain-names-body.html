QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

A clearer next step.

Practical answers before you choose.

Is a domain name the same as web hosting?−

No. A domain is an address, while hosting provides the resources that serve a website. You can connect a domain to a compatible hosting service.

Can you confirm that a name is available?+

A live registrar check is needed to confirm current availability and pricing. The search shown in this design preview does not perform that check.

Can I use my domain for business email?+

Yes, a domain can be connected to a suitable email service. Mailboxes and email hosting are separate from domain registration unless a package explicitly includes them.

Can I transfer a domain I already own?+

Eligibility depends on the domain’s status, extension and applicable transfer rules. Access and authorisation requirements should be checked first.

Is domain privacy available?+

Availability and conditions depend on the extension and registrar. Confirm the privacy options for the particular domain before registration.

What happens if my domain expires?+

Expiry and recovery rules vary. Review renewal notices and the applicable policy; recovery after expiry may involve restrictions or additional fees.
