QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

A clearer next step.

Practical answers before you choose.

What is business email hosting?−

Business email hosting provides mailboxes using your own domain, such as name@yourbrand.example. It is separate from the domain itself and may be separate from website hosting.

Do I need a website to use business email?+

A website is not required, but you need a domain you can manage and an email service connected through the appropriate records.

Can I keep my existing email addresses?+

This may be possible when you control the domain and the new platform supports the required setup. We review the current configuration before confirming the move.

Can existing messages be transferred?+

Migration depends on the source and destination systems, access and supported tools. The scope and handling of existing data must be confirmed before work begins.

Can I use email on my phone or desktop?+

Compatibility depends on the selected platform, supported protocols and applications. Confirm your preferred devices and software when discussing the service.

Does business email guarantee delivery to every inbox?+

No. Delivery depends on several factors, including configuration, reputation, message content and the recipient’s systems. No universal inbox-placement guarantee is made.
